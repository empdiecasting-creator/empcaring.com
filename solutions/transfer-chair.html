What every model shares

Same steel frame, same safety belt, same commode system — whether you pick manual, hydraulic, or electric.

High-carbon steel frame

High-carbon steel main frame, piano white baked paint, fully automatic welding. The frame takes daily use in nursing homes and hospitals without rusting or warping.

Double-buckle backrest

Dual-locking backrest stays put during transfers. Quick-release handles let you take the frame apart for shipping or storage.

Safety belt + cushion

Linen sponge cushion and safety belt keep the resident secure during lifts. Commode bedpan comes with every unit.

Flip-up footboard

Footboard flips up when you need to transfer, shower, or use the commode — flips back down for seated support.

360° maneuverability

Universal rear wheels let the chair turn in place — rotate inside a small bathroom or narrow ward. Corrosion-resistant casters hold up to daily cleaning.

OEM & export ready

FDA certified. Custom branding, colors, and packaging available. Foreign-language manuals and labeling for overseas distributors.
