Choose the right lift mechanism

All three models share the same frame, seat, and safety features. The difference is how height is adjusted — hand-crank, hydraulic pedal, or electric.

Manual Transfer Chair

Hand-crank height adjustment. No power needed — fully mechanical operation.

  • Capacity150 kg
  • Seat height45–63 cm

Hydraulic Transfer Chair

Hydraulic pedal lift. Smooth foot-pedal operation, no electricity needed.

  • Capacity150 kg

Electric hydraulic lift with wireless remote. Push-button height adjustment.

  • Capacity150 kg
