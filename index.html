Choosing a model

Three questions decide the model, not the price

Procurement usually starts with a quote and works backwards. We find it faster to start with the resident and the building, then choose the drive type that fits both.

1. Can the resident sit upright and stay balanced?

If not, a seat-lift transfer chair is the wrong device — that resident needs a sling hoist with a two-person transfer. Our guide to the four machines behind the name explains the split.

2. Who moves them, and how often?

Manual for low volume and no charging routine. Hydraulic where staff prefer a foot pedal to a hand crank. Electric where one caregiver handles a heavy resident several times a day. Compare all three drive types.

3. How wide is the narrowest door on the route?

Our standard frames are 61–53 cm wide; the optional 30-inch (76 cm) base is built for older homes. Measure the door, subtract two inches, then compare. The dimensions guide has the arithmetic.

Questions buyers ask before the first order

If yours is not here, ask it directly — we answer freight, MOQ and documentation questions the same day.

What is your minimum order quantity?

There is no strict minimum. Tiered wholesale pricing starts at 20 units, mixed across models and colours, and a full container simply gives you the lowest freight rate per chair.

Do you sell to families, or only to businesses?

We supply distributors, DME dealers, nursing homes, hospitals and home care agencies, and we run OEM and private-label production for them. Families should buy through a local dealer that can service the chair.

Which of the three models should we order first?

Manual for low volume where nobody wants to maintain a charging routine, hydraulic where staff prefer a foot pedal, electric where one caregiver moves a heavy resident several times a day. Many facilities start with one of each and standardise on what the staff actually reach for.

How long from order to delivery?

Samples ship in about a week. Production runs 30–35 days after deposit and artwork approval, then we book the container and hand over the export documents. Ocean transit is on top of that and depends on the destination port.

Can the chairs carry our brand?

Yes. Colour, logo, carton, manual and label are private-label items matched to a physical sample. Changing the frame geometry or the drive mechanism needs new tooling and a fresh load test, so budget for a longer lead time on those.

Tell us which models and quantities you need — bulk orders and OEM runs welcome. We'll send FDA documentation and a detailed wholesale quote within one business day. No obligation.
