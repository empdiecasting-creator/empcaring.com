Most sourcing conversations start with a price and end with a mismatch: the chair arrives, the frame is wider than the bathroom door, the seat stops 6 cm below the mattress, and the facility's aides go back to two-person lifting. The numbers that prevent that are boring and public, so we publish them.

What we manufacture

How the line runs

Buyers who visit ask the same thing in different words: is this assembled from bought-in parts, or built here? The honest answer is that a transfer chair is a mix — tube, castors, actuators and fasteners are components — and what matters is which steps happen under our roof and which are inspected on arrival.

1. Tube cutting and bending. High-carbon steel tube is cut to length, bent and drilled on jigs, not freehand.

2. Welding. Frame joints are welded in fixture, then dressed. Weld penetration is checked visually on every frame and destructively on samples.

3. Surface finishing. Degrease, phosphate, powder coat, cure. Colour is matched to a physical sample, not to a screen.

4. Assembly. Castors, lift mechanism, seat, backrest and commode bucket fitted; brake function and seat travel measured against the spec sheet.

5. Load and function test. Static load at rated capacity, lift cycle test, and a full up-down cycle with the unit loaded before packing.

Five documents you can request before ordering

If a supplier cannot produce these, the price on the quotation is not comparable to ours — it is a different product with less testing behind it.

  • Dimensional drawing with tolerances, not just a marketing spec table.
  • Static load-test report at 150 kg, with the test rig described.
  • FDA registration and device listing details so the buyer can check the establishment and the listing themselves.
  • Incoming inspection standard for the components we buy in — castors, actuators, gas struts.
  • Packaging and drop-test summary, because a chair that arrives bent is a return, not a delivery.

MOQ, lead time and terms

  • MOQ: one container, mixed across models and colours.
  • Samples: available before an order, typically shipping within a week.
  • Production: 30–35 days after deposit and artwork approval.
  • Terms: 30% deposit, balance against copy of the bill of lading.
  • Ports: FOB Shenzhen or Guangzhou; CIF quoted on request.
  • Spare parts: commode buckets, castors, actuators and manuals are shipped separately on request — welded frame damage is not a part swap, which is why the packaging spec matters.

On certifications, including the one we do not have

EMPCaring is FDA registered, and the models are device-listed. We are not FDA approved or FDA certified, because the FDA does not issue certifications for this device class — any supplier telling you otherwise is either confused or hoping you are. We also do not hold ISO 13485 for these models, and we will not present a supplier's ISO certificate as our own. If a tender requires it, tell us and we will scope what qualification would actually involve.

What we can document today: registration and listing references, load-test reports, dimensional drawings, incoming inspection records and packaging test summaries. For the wider context on what US buyers must hold to sell this equipment, see DME licensing and Medicare enrollment.

Who we already ship to

DME distributors and online retailers in the United States, nursing home groups buying for their own buildings, home care agencies standardising equipment across carers, and importers in other markets who private-label the line. What they have in common is that someone on their side measures a doorway and a mattress height before ordering — which is the whole reason this page exists.

FAQ

Are you a manufacturer or a trading company?

A manufacturer. Frames are cut, welded, coated, assembled and load tested in our facility in Zhaoqing, Guangdong. We also handle export documentation, which is why some buyers assume otherwise.

What is the minimum order quantity?

One container for standard models, mixed across drive types. Samples are available before that.

Can you produce under our brand?

Yes — colour, logo, manual, carton and label. Geometry and lift mechanism changes need tooling and a separate schedule.

What certifications do you hold?

FDA registration and device listing. No ISO 13485 and no CE mark for these models, and we do not describe the products as FDA approved.

How long is lead time?

30–35 days after deposit and artwork approval. Sampling about a week. FOB Shenzhen or Guangzhou, CIF on request.

Ask for the documents, not the brochure

Tell us the model you are replacing, the door width and the mattress height, and we will send the drawing, the load-test report and a landed-cost estimate for a sample order. Manufacturing in Zhaoqing, China; 150 kg rated; FDA registered and device listed; OEM and private label available.
